A focused starting stack

  1. Learn patterns: NeetCode or Take U Forward as your DSA roadmap.
  2. Practise daily: LeetCode, 1–2 problems, reviewing every solution you miss.
  3. Build speed weekly: one Codeforces or CodeChef contest under time pressure.
  4. Go deep monthly: CSES or AtCoder for systematic algorithm practice.
  5. Cover AI/ML and data: Kaggle for real datasets, Deep-ML for implementing ML from scratch, DataLemur for SQL.

You do not need every platform. Pick four or five, practise consistently, and judge progress by problems you can solve cold, not by solved counts.
